Granite Local

Granite Local is a personal, self-hosted assistant that helps its owner manage their own Google Calendar, Gmail and Google Tasks. It runs on the owner's home computer using an open-source AI language model (IBM Granite, run through Ollama and Open WebUI). It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public.

What the app does

  • Calendar: reads the owner's upcoming Google Calendar events so the owner can ask questions like "What's on my schedule tomorrow?", and creates new events when the owner asks it to.
  • Gmail: lists and reads the owner's recent emails so the owner can ask for a summary of their inbox, and saves email drafts for the owner to review. The app never sends email; the owner sends drafts themselves from Gmail.
  • Tasks: lists the owner's Google Tasks, adds new to-dos with an optional due date, and marks tasks as done when the owner asks.

How Google data is used

The app uses Google sign-in (OAuth) so the owner can grant access to their own account. Google data is processed only on the owner's own computer to answer the owner's requests. It is not sold, shared, sent to third parties, or used to train AI models. See the privacy policy for details.

What the app does not do

Granite Local works only with text from the owner's calendar, email and tasks. It does not create, edit or process images, and it is never used to generate sexual, intimate or non-consensual imagery of any kind.
