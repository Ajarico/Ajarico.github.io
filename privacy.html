Granite Local privacy policy

Effective date: November 25, 2026

This policy explains what Google user data Granite Local ("the app") accesses, how it is used, stored and protected, and how access can be removed. Granite Local is a personal, self-hosted assistant with a single user, its owner. It runs on the owner's own computer and is not available to anyone else.

1. Data the app accesses

With the owner's explicit consent through Google OAuth, the app requests these permissions:

  • Google Calendar, read-only (calendar.readonly): event titles, dates, times, locations and descriptions on the owner's calendars, plus the calendar's time zone.
  • Google Calendar, events the app creates (calendar.events.owned): to create calendar events when the owner asks.
  • Gmail, read-only (gmail.readonly): message senders, subjects, dates and message bodies of the owner's emails.
  • Gmail, compose (gmail.compose): to create email drafts when the owner asks. The app does not send email.
  • Google Tasks (tasks): to list the owner's task lists and tasks, create tasks, and mark tasks as done when the owner asks.

The app does not access contacts, Google Drive files, location, payment information or any data belonging to other Google users.

2. How the data is used

Google data is used only to provide the app's features to its owner: answering the owner's questions about their schedule and inbox, summarizing emails, creating calendar events, managing tasks and saving email drafts that the owner requests. Data is processed by an AI language model that runs locally on the owner's computer. It is not sent to any outside AI service.

Google data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to develop, improve or train AI or machine-learning models.

3. Sharing and disclosure

The app does not share, transfer or disclose Google user data to any third party. No other people or organizations have access to the data.

4. Storage and retention

The app stores only the OAuth access and refresh tokens needed to connect to Google, in a file on the owner's computer that only the owner can read. Calendar and email content is fetched from Google when the owner makes a request and is used to answer that request. It is not copied into a separate database or kept long term.

5. Security

The app runs on the owner's private computer and is not reachable from the internet. Connections to Google use encrypted HTTPS, and OAuth tokens are kept in a file with access restricted to the owner.

6. Removing access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the app from reading any further Google data, and the owner can delete the stored token file at any time.

7. AI-generated imagery

The app works only with text. It does not generate or process images and is never used to create sexual, intimate or non-consensual imagery.

8. Google API Services User Data Policy

Granite Local's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the app's owner through github.com/Ajarico.

Terms of service
